> **Target Audience:** Local SMB Owners (Salons, Restaurants, Cafes, Spas, Gyms, Clinics)  
> **Target Profile:** 3.5★ – 4.3★ rating, under 80 Google reviews, trailing top area competitors.

---

## 💡 What We Offer (Product Summary for Sales Reps)

**ReviewFlow** is a **QR-based Google Review Growth & Reputation Management System** designed for local Indian SMBs.

### The 3 Core Benefits to Pitch:
1. **Convert Walk-In Foot Traffic into 5-Star Google Reviews:** Turn everyday happy customers into Google reviews in 10 seconds via custom QR Standees at checkout or dining tables.
2. **Private Feedback Shield (Negative Review Interceptor):** If a customer is unhappy (<4 stars), ReviewFlow routes their feedback to a private owner form instead of posting publicly on Google Maps.
3. **Outrank Nearby Competitors on Google Maps:** More 5-star reviews = higher Google Maps local rank = 3x more new customer calls and foot traffic.
